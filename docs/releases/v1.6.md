Set a daily goal for each project and get a recap every time you stop. Works offline; your data stays on your phone.

## Install

1. Download **TimeTracker.apk** below on your Android phone (Android 7 or newer).
2. Open it and allow installs from your browser if Android asks.
3. Already have the app? Choose **Update**. Don't uninstall first, because that deletes your data.

## What's new in 1.6

- **Daily goal:** set minutes per day in *Edit project* (Off, 15–120 min or a custom number). A ring next to the project name fills as you track and turns solid when you reach it, with a "Daily goal met" toast once a day. Tap the ring to open Statistics.
- **Session recap:** stopping a session of a minute or more shows what it added, how close you are to today's goal, and up to two highlights: a new longest session, a new day record, your busiest day this week, your streak, or a new 10-hour mark. **Do it again** starts the same task.
- **Weekly recap:** at the start of each week, a card sums up last week until you dismiss it: total and change from the week before, goal days, best day, top activity and sessions. Open it any time from **Last week's recap** on Statistics.
- **Goal streaks:** a day with a goal counts toward your streak only if you met it. Your best streak is always shown.
- **Statistics:** a new **Goal days** tile for the last 30 days, and "goal met" in the activity calendar.

Projects without a goal look and work exactly as before.
