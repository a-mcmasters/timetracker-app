A time tracker for language learning: time your immersion, tag what you're doing, and watch your streak grow. Works offline; your data stays on your phone.

## Install

1. Download **TimeTracker.apk** below on your Android phone (Android 7 or newer).
2. Open it and allow installs from your browser if Android asks.
3. Already have the app? Choose **Update**. Don't uninstall first, because that deletes your data.

## What's in 1.5

- **Smart task names:** pick tags and the name writes itself, following English grammar: *Watching YouTube*, *Reading a book*, *Listening to an audiobook*, *Reading the news*, *Speaking with a tutor*.
- **Activity and content tags:** activities (Watching, Listening, Reading, Mining…) come first, content (YouTube, Podcast, Book…) after.
- **Timer:** start, pause and stop, with a floating mini timer. It keeps counting while the app is closed.
- **Statistics:**
  - Total time, sessions, longest session and day record.
  - The last 30 days, colour-coded by activity.
  - Time per tag.
- **Activity calendar:** a GitHub-style heatmap of the last 12 months or any year, with your current and longest streak.
- **Projects:** one per language, each with its own colour (e.g. orange for Dutch). The statistics use that colour.
- **History:** grouped by day, with continue, edit and delete, plus manual entries and CSV export.
